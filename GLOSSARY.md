# educates.dev

The public website of the Educates project: what Educates is, who it is for,
and everything the project publishes about it.

## Language

**Content**:
Anything the Educates project publishes for its audience: blog posts,
getting started guides, about pages, videos and talks, wherever they are
hosted.
_Avoid_: Blog (when meaning all content), resources, articles

**Featured Content**:
The hand-picked subset of Content that the site promotes on its homepage,
drawn from any kind of Content.
_Avoid_: Highlights, featured posts (Featured Content is not limited to posts)

**Content hub**:
The one page that lists Content of every kind, filterable by kind and
Topic, with Featured Content pinned at the top. The blog's own list is a
separate, posts-only view.
_Avoid_: Resources page, library

**Topic**:
A curated subject used to filter the Content hub, defined as a set of
blog tags. Content carries tags; a Topic groups them.
_Avoid_: Tag (when meaning the curated filter), category

**Blog post**:
A dated, authored article published on the site's blog. One kind of Content.
_Avoid_: Article, news

## Product

**Feature**:
A capability of Educates that someone can see and use, such as the
workshop terminal or clickable actions, grouped by the job it serves:
authoring, delivering or operating workshops.
_Avoid_: Benefit, value claim (such as "Secure by Design")

**Use case**:
A job a team does with Educates, presented on its own page of the site. A
use case combines several Features; it is not a Feature itself.
_Avoid_: Solution

## Demos

**Demo Platform**:
A company's own system for giving its field teams one-click access to
curated demos and workshops of its products, with Educates as its core.
Educates is the core, not the whole platform. Educates is ready to use as
it is for an internal Demo Platform; a public-facing one adds its own front
end and integrations on top.
_Avoid_: Demo environment (Educates already uses "workshop environment" for
something else), sandbox platform

**Session**:
One isolated copy of a workshop, given to a single Presenter or Attendee,
which can be prepared ahead of time so it starts instantly.
_Avoid_: Sandbox, demo environment, instance

**Demo**:
A workshop that a Presenter delivers to Attendees. A way of delivering a
workshop, not a separate kind of thing.
_Avoid_: Demo workshop (redundant), showcase

**Hands-on event**:
A one-off event with a set time and known Attendees, such as a conference
workshop, a customer workshop or a booth, where each Attendee works in their
own Session.
_Avoid_: Product demo (blurs it with Demo)

## Demo roles

**Sponsor**:
The person who funds a company's use of Educates for demos, typically the
head of sales engineering or field enablement. Not someone who funds the
Educates project itself: the site calls that "supporting the project".
_Avoid_: Buyer, customer

**Builder**:
The team inside a company that builds and runs that company's demos and
workshops on Educates, typically a platform or DevRel team.
_Avoid_: Admin, operator (when meaning the whole team)

**Presenter**:
The sales or solutions engineer who launches a demo in front of Attendees.
_Avoid_: Instructor, SE

**Attendee**:
A person at a demo or event who watches or takes part in a workshop.
_Avoid_: Audience (that word means the site's readers), learner, user
