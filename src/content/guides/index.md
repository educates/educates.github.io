These guides take you from an empty laptop to a workshop of your own,
running on Educates on your machine. Follow them in order: each part builds
on the one before.

1. [Set up](/getting-started-guides/setup): install Docker, kubectl and the
   Educates CLI, then create a local Educates cluster with one command.
2. [What you just installed](/getting-started-guides/about): the pods,
   policies and resources that cluster runs, and the commands to look at
   them.
3. [Write your first workshop](/getting-started-guides/authoring): generate
   a workshop and deploy it, prepare each Session with setup scripts, guide
   the work with clickable actions, and edit the instructions live.
4. [Next steps](/getting-started-guides/next-steps): deploy ready-made
   workshops from the Hub, find the reference docs, and get help.
